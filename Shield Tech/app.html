            What this app stores

            • Your account: name, email and optional phone, in this browser's storage.
            • Your password: as a salted hash, never as the words you typed.
            • Your checks: every message you scanned, so History can reopen it.
            • Your reports and votes: part of the community record other people rely on.
            • Attachments you added: images and documents, with image metadata stripped.

            None of it is sent anywhere. There is no server behind this build, which also means clearing your browser data erases it and nobody can recover it for you.
